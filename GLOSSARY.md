# Personal homepage

The public homepage of Yu Hao (zhyfeng.github.io): a Jekyll site presenting research, publications and CV. This glossary covers the words used when deciding how the site presents its owner.

## Language

**Visual identity**:
The look that makes the site recognisably this person's and not a template: typography, colour, motif, layout rhythm. The current project is about this.
_Avoid_: 个性化, design, theme, style

**Voice**:
The way the written text sounds: first person or third, story or summary, warm or formal. Out of scope for the current project.
_Avoid_: tone, copy, 文案

**Hero**:
The first screen of the homepage: the rail (photo, 郝宇 mark, table of contents, profile links) and the Name, Description and Facts sections beside it.
_Avoid_: banner, header, intro

**Fact strip**:
The compact line of facts in the Hero computed from publication data (count, venues, awards) instead of typed by hand.
_Avoid_: fact line, stats, highlights

**Variant**:
One of several structurally different renderings of the homepage built during prototyping to pick the Visual identity.
_Avoid_: version, theme, mockup
